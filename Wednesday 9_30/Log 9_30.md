 ## Say Hello to Pong!
 - I switched gears to a pong game because I already have an interest in game design and I felt there was an intersting 3d I could play with instead of standard 2D pong.
 - The result is a tunnel with a webcam in the bottom right that tracks your head movement as the paddle to play a game of pong.
 - Prompting was simple at first and I now have to tweak sensitivity and overall comfort of play.
 - I'm also curious about setting up a lobby system to allow 2 player gamees but that will come much later.
 - I played around with visuals style and I think that is the next step. I like the "tron" look the most so far but have also tried a frutiger aero style.

## Claude's Timeline
- Base game. Wireframe tunnel (4 × 3 × 12), ball with spin/curve physics, player and CPU paddles, depth-marker ring, glow, HUD, levels and lives. Mouse control through the container's Panel CHOP; F1 opens it via /perform. Fixed triangle diagonals in the tunnel (wireframe "topology" mode).
- Head control. Hooked up the existing pose bridge's nose landmark. Mirrored mapping, smoothing, automatic fallback to mouse. Fixed the OSC CHOP holding stale values after the bridge stops (a frozen nose counts as "not tracked").
- Webcam view. Mirrored picture-in-picture, reloaded only when the bridge writes a new frame, hidden when frames go stale. Nose dot + rest ring overlay. Fixed a border-width unit bug that turned the ring into a solid blob.
- Head-only start. Replaced click / countdown with "hold your nose in the ring" to serve and restart; new-player detection resets calibration. Moved prompts above the ball.
- Sound. Generated retro sounds: player / CPU hits, wall tick (quieter further down the tunnel), score, miss, level up, game over.
- Sensitivity tuning over several rounds (see current values below); ring pinned to the webcam centre, then shrunk and lowered to where your nose naturally rests.
- Frutiger Aero look. Sky, sun, clouds, hills, bokeh, glossy bubbles, glass paddles, orb ball, glossy HUD in Lucida Grande. Fixed washed-out compositing (camera fog alpha was tinting the transparent background).
- Tron look. Neon grid tunnel, identity-disc ball, bracketed neon paddles, angled HUD in DIN Alternate, two-stage bloom, T-key switching. Light cycles were built, then removed at your request. Depth marker rebuilt as a solid camera-facing light frame (macOS ignores line widths above 1px, so thin rings vanished). Grid now fades to a new colour each level.
- Player-set calibration. Hold still elsewhere to move the ring; C key places it instantly.
- Pause. Look away (head mode) or P to pause; centre in the ring or P / click to resume with a countdown; 45 s empty pause resets the game.
