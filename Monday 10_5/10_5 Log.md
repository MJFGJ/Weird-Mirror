# Pong is here to stay!
The pong prototype has been a great change for the project. The Functionality of it all is pretty well tuned there are some things with the flow that can be spruced up as things appear rather fast and currently users learn on the fly. The first few points always feel like the most frustrating as users figure out how exactly it all plays.

## User Testing
Amber 
- Confused as to how it worked for a while. Paddle movement was tough for her due to unfamiliarty. 
- Game started too quick for her and took her by surprise the first time.
- Afterward she was moving her head out of the ring a lot so there became some trial and error. 
- Depth was hard to gauge.
- Paddle movement felt inconsistent. 
- Did not pay attention to anything on the top bar, was just trying to return the ball as much as possible. 
- Never made it to round 2.

Jay 
- Watched Amber play so had some idea of what to expect. 
- Turned more into a competition. I felt this was good because He never brought up that it didnt feel fair or awkward he just kept trying to play and get to round 2 which he did once. 
- He did notice the lives put didn't actively pay attention. 
- Might not be enough time in between points? 

Taryn 
- Just stood there was completely lost for a bit. I had to instruct her to at least move to see if anything changed. 
- Took her a while to pick it up. Definitely had trouble moving the paddle to areas she wanted. 
- Used bigger movements than needed which made her overshoot by quite a bit. 
- Said she would like to use her hands. 
- Again first couple points were a learning experience and she seemed unready for it to begin. 
- Lots of "oh no" and laughs. 

## Things I Noticed
- Top bar seemed to show more info than needed. No one cares about score just lives and level. 
- The instruction text seems weirdly easy to miss. It's pretty big but might need to be something more eyecatching. 
- Game start definitley happens too quickly and makes people scramble. 
- I wouldn't mind some sort of reset cue.

# Things Implemented Since Users
- Changed the CPU lives to orange
- Added a countdown between points and at the game start, and then removed the countdown between points. Felt too much.
- Tuned a little bit of the control sensitivity.
- Have game flash orange when you lose a point to match CPU color.
- Levels change the grid color.
- Game start now begins with large webcam screen to help players recognize whats happening. 
- Removed unneeded info from the topbar like score. Users didn't care they just wanted to get to the next level.
- Made the nose ring slightly bigger and easier for people to notice.
- Lives reset after getting to next level.
