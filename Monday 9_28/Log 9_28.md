## Personal Description
I was able to achieve a more desired effect after I scraped the masking and asked for the photos to just be overlayed on top. Once we had this the effect was extremely laggy so I needed to reduce the size of the images which claude was able to do. Then I felt the effect was somewhat stale so I added movement "jumping" to the images to make things appear more dynamic. All of these things worked without much fuss. What has become hard for me to apply is it's ability to fit to the human shape. A lot of the time effect makes you look like a blob of images rather than you're actual form. This will be what I try to tackle next. before then moving on to interaction. I'm still trying to figure out something alternative to tapping the images because currently the effect appears to have trouble figuring out "tapping/presing" photos when you're whole body is made up of them.

The effect appears to be coming along I think with more photos it will be much more interesting but for now I think progress has been made.
